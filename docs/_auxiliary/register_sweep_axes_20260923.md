# Register sweeps and axis columns (proposal)

**Status:** in-progress
**Closes when:** consumed by the staleness/sweep process pass after Pass B
**Last updated:** 2026-09-24

Art's proposal, captured verbatim in Claude Code's scratchpad on 2026-09-23 (pass
20260922) and routed here by close amendment CA18f. Unvetted. Related design question:
`docs/registers/open_questions.md` Q5.

> Register files in docs/registers/ (e.g. open_questions.md and deferred.md) and
> temporary files in docs/_auxiliary/ (e.g. ideas_20260922.md,
> piece3_design_20260922.md) will need to be swept routinely to identify items and/or
> files of interest based on the current project stage/piece. Related, registers and
> temporary files may need one or more content-specific axis columns (e.g. discussed,
> resolved, vetted, migrated, scoped, implemented, deprecated, etc.) to track/update
> each item's status(es) and subsequent fate (e.g. an idea being discussed, vetted, and
> migrated to a deferred item or discussed, vetted, and scoped for implementation in a
> specific stage/piece; an open question being discussed, resolved, and added to
> DECISIONS.md; a deferred item being discussed, scoped for implementation in a specific
> stage/piece, and implemented successfully or discussed and deprecated or discussed and
> remain deferred until a later sweep; etc. etc.). The lists of potential axis columns
> and hypothetical scenarios are just ideas and not exhaustive; details will need to be
> worked out in future passes.
